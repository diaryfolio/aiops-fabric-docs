# Is ViewSense a fit for your team?

ViewSense AI® is intended for work that combines information from several company systems,
uses AI to help analyse it, and returns a useful outcome to the right people.
Start with a focused reporting or investigation pilot that a person can review.

## Product fit at a glance

| A useful first pilot has… | Example |
|---|---|
| A clear business question | How have reorganisation departures and department performance changed over six months? |
| Information spread across systems | HR records, department structures and Finance reports. |
| A defined audience | A senior manager receiving a department-level summary. |
| An outcome someone can check | A report with charts, source references and a clear account of missing information. |

The [examples](value-and-use-cases.md) cover business reporting and technical investigations.
They describe proposed workflows; the connectors and complete business execution still need
implementation and acceptance for the chosen pilot.

## The workflow at a glance

1. **Choose one outcome**

    Name the question, the people who need the answer and the person accountable for it.

2. **Agree the boundaries**

    Select approved information, a limited user group and a human reviewer.

3. **Decide what success means**

    Agree how to assess accuracy, usefulness, turnaround time and cost before expanding.

## What is ready today?

The current reference demonstrates individual components and a fixed synthetic execution check.
It does not yet deliver the complete business workflows described in the examples.
Some integrations need configuration and testing; others are planned work.

Ask the technical team to confirm the required connections and permissions before committing to
a pilot. Product choices, hosting requirements and technical readiness are documented in
[Integration compatibility and acceptance](../design/high-level/10-overall/11-integration-compatibility.md)
under Architecture.

For ownership, review and approval decisions, continue to [Adoption and governance](adoption-and-governance.md).
